Measurement tool

 

Toolbar:   

 

Menu: CAD-Earth > Mesh commands > Mesh Viewer

 

Command line: CE_MESHVIEWER

Mesh Viewer toolbar:

CAD-Earth version: Premium

 

Command description:

 

This tools allows you to measure the 2D/3D distance, height difference, slope and XY angle between to points. You can measure points directly on the surface, along the X, Y or Z axis, and on the XY, XZ or YZ planes. A temporary line will be drawn between the two points to show their position and direction.

 

 

 

 

Tips:

 

•You can move, rotate or pan the camera to get a better view when selecting points.

•If you press the Clear button or close the dialog box, the temporary line will disappear.
