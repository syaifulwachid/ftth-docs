Apply license

 

Toolbar:   

 

Menu: CAD-Earth > License Management > Apply license

 

Command line: CE_APPLYLICENSE

 

CAD-Earth version:  Basic, Plus, Premium.

 

Command description:

 

Starting with CAD-Earth 6.0, all licenses are of the floating type. This means you can apply the license at any computer and use CAD-Earth as long as there's an available license from the pool.

For example, if you bought just one license, the first computer you use will take the available license, and if you need to use CAD-Earth in another computer, you must first close AutoCAD or do a license checkout in the computer that is using the license. If you bought a 5-license pack and you have applied the license to 10 computers, only the first 5 users will be able to use CAD-Earth, and then you will have to wait until somebody does a license checkout or close AutoCAD to get the available license.

 

When you apply the license, you will be asked to enter your email, username and password from your CAD-Earth License Activation Center account. This information was sent to you by email after completing a CAD-Earth license purchase or provided by your CAD-Earth dealer. If you forgot your password, the email and username must be supplied to get the password sent to the user's email. The email and username have to be the same as in your CAD-Earth account. You can select 'Go to your account' to change or see your email, but you have to enter the correct username and password to be able to access your account.

 

 

 

 

After you press the Sign In button, a 6-digit code will be sent to the user's email. If you don't receive this code after 2 or 3 minutes, please check the spam or junk mailbox. This code expires after 10 minutes, if you don't get the 6-digit code at this time interval, please contact us at support@cad-earth.com or submit a support ticket.

 

If you have more than 1 CAD-Earth license type in your account (Basic, Plus, 1 Year, Permanent, etc) you must select from the list the license type you wish to apply.

 

 

 

 

You must get a 'Successfully signed to user's account' message to confirm the license was applied correctly after entering the 6-digit code. If you get an error message, please contact us at support@cad-earth.com or submit a support ticket.

 

 

 

Tips:

 

•The CAD-Earth demo version license can't be activated. You must install the CAD-Earth registered version to apply the license. The links to download the CAD-Earth registered version were sent to your email after completing a CAD-Earth license purchase or provided by your CAD-Earth dealer.

•You can use the CAD-Earth license information command to see which licenses are available.

•If you suspect someone is using a license from your account, you can go to your CAD-Earth account and change your password and apply the license again using this command.

•You must have an Internet connection to apply the license and to use most of CAD-Earth's commands.
